KARUVI(கருவி)

Together we save. Together we support.

Karuvi is a shared community fund built through regular member contributions, transparent records, and collective support when it matters. It brings members together to build a dependable pool of funds through consistent giving. Every entry is recorded clearly so the community can understand how funds are received, maintained, and used. The goal is simple: shared responsibility, timely help, and lasting trust among members.
